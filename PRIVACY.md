# Privacy Policy for Headphone Control

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Headphone Control does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- **Bluetooth** is used only to communicate with your Soundcore Sleep A30 earbuds. Nothing about
  your earbuds or your phone leaves the device.
- **Local storage** holds only what the app needs to reconnect quickly: the earbuds' Bluetooth
  identifier and service identifiers, your developer-mode preference, and any diagnostic
  snapshots you choose to capture. All of it stays on your phone and can be cleared with
  Settings › Forget earbuds.
- **Feedback** is sent through your own mail app, only when you tap Send and choose to send it.
  The draft includes a diagnostic log that may contain your earbuds' serial number and
  Bluetooth identifiers. You can remove the attachment before sending.
- The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.
